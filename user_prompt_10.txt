8. Transparent buyer cost and seller earnings

Use a consistent calculation throughout the application.

Buyer total cost:

Purchased energy × energy price + applicable buyer charges.

Seller gross revenue:

Sold energy × agreed energy price.

Seller net earnings:

Gross revenue − seller-paid fees and applicable deductions.

Buyer estimated savings:

Comparable baseline cost for the same energy quantity − total buyer cost.

Savings percentage:

Estimated savings ÷ comparable baseline cost × 100.

For example, for 5 kWh:

- Illustrative baseline: ₹8/kWh.
- Energy price: ₹4.20/kWh.
- Illustrative additional charges: ₹0.20/kWh.

Baseline cost = ₹40.

Buyer cost = ₹22.

Estimated buyer savings = ₹18, or 45%.

This is an example, not a promise of real savings. Configure the baseline to represent the relevant comparison tariff. Do not claim savings if a valid baseline is unavailable or the result is negative.

Use the same calculation service for dashboards, transaction confirmations, and analytics.
